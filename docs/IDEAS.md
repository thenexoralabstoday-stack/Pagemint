# Websites you can build next to earn money

Ranked by how well they fit what you already have (browser-side processing, Stripe billing, Claude AI backend). Each one reuses the Pagemint shell: landing, tool grid, free/pro gating, checkout.

## Tier 1 — same playbook, proven demand

1. **Image toolbox** (compress, resize, convert HEIC/WEBP/AVIF, remove background, upscale). Search volume is larger than PDF. Background removal runs in-browser with a small ONNX model; upscaling can be a Pro server feature. Monetise like Pagemint: 3 free/day, $7/mo Pro.
2. **ClipForge — short-form video maker** (the second project in this workspace). Long video in, captioned 9:16 clips out. Creators pay $19–49/mo for this (Opus Clip, Vizard, Klap). Costs are real (GPU/transcription), so price higher and meter by minutes of video.
3. **Video toolbox** (compress, trim, convert, GIF maker, subtitle burner). ffmpeg.wasm makes this client-side for files under ~500 MB. Same pricing as PDF.
4. **Audio tools** (transcribe, remove noise, convert, podcast trimmer). Transcription via Whisper is cheap and people pay per hour of audio.

## Tier 2 — AI document products (Claude backend you already have)

5. **Resume + cover letter builder** with ATS scoring. Users paste a job ad, get a tailored resume PDF. $19 one-time per download or $9/mo. High intent, high willingness to pay.
6. **Contract reviewer** for freelancers and small businesses: upload a contract, get risks highlighted. Position as "not legal advice". $29 per review or $19/mo.
7. **Invoice and quote generator** with recurring invoices and payment links (Stripe Connect). Free to create, $9/mo to remove branding and automate reminders.
8. **AI study tools**: PDF or lecture in, flashcards, quizzes and summaries out. Students pay $5–8/mo in large numbers; sell yearly at back-to-school.
9. **Meeting notes / transcript summariser** for Zoom and Teams recordings.

## Tier 3 — utility sites with ads or micro-payments

10. **QR code generator with tracking** (dynamic QR codes). Free static codes, $5/mo for dynamic ones with scan analytics. Classic recurring revenue.
11. **E-signature lite**: send a PDF for signature, audit trail, no per-envelope pricing. Undercut DocuSign at $8/mo unlimited.
12. **File transfer** (WeTransfer clone) with end-to-end encryption. Storage costs, so charge from day one: $5/mo for 50 GB.
13. **Screenshot and mockup beautifier**, **favicon generator**, **social image maker**: small tools that rank fast, monetise with a single Pro tier or ads.

## Tier 4 — bigger bets

14. **Vertical PDF products**: "PDF tools for real estate agents" (lease templates, e-sign, watermark), "for teachers" (worksheet maker, grading), "for lawyers" (redaction, Bates numbering, bundling). Same tools, ten times the price, less competition.
15. **White-label / API**: sell the Pagemint engine to other websites as an embeddable widget or REST API. $49–299/mo.
16. **Chrome extension bundle**: a paid extension pack (PDF, screenshots, tab manager). Extensions are their own distribution channel.

## How to pick

- Search volume you can win: use Google Keyword Planner or Ahrefs free tools for "X online free". PDF and image tools have millions of monthly searches; niches have thousands but convert at 5–10x.
- Cost of goods: prefer tools that run in the browser (near-zero cost) unless customers will pay $20+ per month.
- Reuse: everything here shares the same billing server, auth and landing template you now have. Building the second product takes a fraction of the time of the first.

## Monetisation patterns that work in 2026

- Freemium with a daily task cap plus a one-week pass.
- Yearly default with 30–40% discount.
- Lifetime deal at launch (AppSumo, own site) for cash and reviews.
- Affiliate program at 30% recurring.
- Sponsorship slots on free tools once traffic passes ~50k/month (Carbon Ads, EthicalAds, direct).
- Sell to teams once individuals are converting; seats are where the money is.
